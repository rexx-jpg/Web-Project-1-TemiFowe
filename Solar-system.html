The Solar System

The solar system is a gravitationally bound star system containing the Sun, eight planets, moons, asteroids, and comets. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

How It Formed

The formation of the solar system began when a giant molecular cloud collapsed under its own gravity,most of the material was pulled toward the center, forming the Sun. The remaining protoplanetary disk of gas and dust eventually coalesced into the planets, moons astriods and other proto-planetary bodies.
