The Solar System

The solar system is a gravitationally bound star system containing the Sun, eight planets, moons, asteroids, and comets. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

How It Formed

The formation of the solar system began when a giant molecular cloud collapsed under its own gravity, creating the Sun at the center while leftover material formed planets and other celestial bodies.
