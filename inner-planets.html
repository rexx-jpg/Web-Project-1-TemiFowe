the inner include Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. These planets are also known as the terrestrial planets because they have solid, rocky surfaces. They are located closer to the Sun than the outer planets,which include Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The inner planets are smaller in size and have fewer moons compared to the outer planets.
